A game that stays on your iPhone.

Ghospath | Memory Game, provided by Debugger Society, works offline without an account. The app saves your best score and preferences, including sound, haptics, and reduced effects, using local storage on your device. It does not transmit this information to us.

Scores do not sync between devices. Deleting the app removes its local data. There is no server-side player profile to delete, and we cannot recover a score that was never sent to us.

No advertising or tracking.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs. It does not request access to contacts, photos, location, a microphone, or advertising identifiers.

If you choose to email us.

When you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as your message, device details, or a screenshot. We use that correspondence to respond and troubleshoot. This is separate from the game, which does not automatically send us information.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to address your request or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing debuggersociety@zohomail.com.

About this website.

This site does not set its own cookies or include analytics, advertising scripts, or contact forms. Its pages and images are served as static files. When hosted on GitHub Pages, the hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site under its own privacy policy.

Questions are welcome.

For privacy questions or requests, contact debuggersociety@zohomail.com.

If the app’s behavior changes, this policy will be updated to describe the new version.
