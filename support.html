Good questions.

How do I play?

Tap Play. Watch the numbered route appear, then retrace it from the mint starting tile. Select the whole route in order, then tap Check. Moves go up, down, left, or right without revisiting a tile. Undo and Clear let you edit before submission. Levels continue until you lose three hearts; paths grow to 21 moves and reveals get faster.

How do points work?

Only a completely correct submitted route earns points: 10 per move. Finishing a level adds 60 points plus 2 points per whole second remaining. A trail with no mistakes or glimpses earns another 40 points. Consecutive clean trails add 15, 30, then up to 45 points. There is no final level or completion bonus.

What does another glimpse cost?

A glimpse costs 40 points you have already earned. It reveals the full route for 2.2 seconds while your recall timer pauses. Your unsubmitted answer is preserved afterward. Using a glimpse breaks your clean-trail streak. The button is unavailable when you have fewer than 40 points.

What happens if I make a mistake or run out of time?

Selecting a tile never tells you whether it is correct and never removes a heart. A wrong complete submission costs one heart. Running out of time also costs a heart. If hearts remain, the correct route appears briefly before a fresh path starts at the same level. Three lost hearts end the journey.

Why can’t I hear the game?

Check Sound in the app’s settings, your iPhone’s media volume, and Silent Mode. Ghostpath respects Silent Mode and pauses for audio interruptions. Haptics are optional and depend on supported hardware.

Where are my score and settings saved?

They are stored locally on your iPhone and remain when you close and reopen the app. They do not sync between devices. Deleting the app removes its local data. An unfinished journey starts over if the app is fully closed; simply switching away pauses it.

Do I need internet, an account, or a subscription?

No. The game works offline, has no sign-in, no ads, and no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Emailing support uses your mail app and an internet connection.

Which devices are supported?

Ghostpath is made for iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later.
