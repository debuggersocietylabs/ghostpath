Good questions.

How do I play?

Tap Follow the light. Watch the numbered route appear, then retrace it from the mint starting tile. Tap the next tile in order; moves go up, down, left, or right. The paths get longer across seven trails. Finish all seven, or begin a fresh journey after losing three hearts.

How do points work?

Each correct step earns 10 points. Finishing a trail earns 60 points plus 2 points per whole second remaining. A trail with no mistakes or glimpses earns another 40 points. Consecutive clean trails add 15, 30, then up to 45 points. Completing the seventh trail adds a 200-point journey bonus.

What does another glimpse cost?

A glimpse costs 40 points you have already earned. It reveals the full route for 2.2 seconds while your recall timer pauses. You continue from your current tile afterward. Using a glimpse breaks your clean-trail streak. The button is unavailable when you have fewer than 40 points.

What happens if I make a mistake or run out of time?

A wrong adjacent tile costs one heart, but you stay on your last correct tile. Tapping a distant tile does not move you. Running out of time also costs a heart; if you have hearts left, the route is shown again and the recall timer resets. Three lost hearts end the journey.

Why can’t I hear the game?

Check Sound in the app’s settings, your iPhone’s media volume, and Silent Mode. Ghospath respects Silent Mode and pauses for audio interruptions. Haptics are optional and depend on supported hardware.

Where are my score and settings saved?

They are stored locally on your iPhone and remain when you close and reopen the app. They do not sync between devices. Deleting the app removes its local data. An unfinished journey starts over if the app is fully closed; simply switching away pauses it.

Do I need internet, an account, or a subscription?

No. The game works offline, has no sign-in, no ads, and no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Emailing support uses your mail app and an internet connection.

Which devices are supported?

Ghospath is made for iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later.
